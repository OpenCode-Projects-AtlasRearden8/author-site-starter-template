About Dylan

I spent years working as a registered nurse, where a clear explanation can genuinely change someone's day — or their outcome. When AI tools arrived, I kept noticing the same problem: enormously useful software wrapped in instructions written for engineers.

So I became an AI author. My books explain AI the way I would explain it to a friend at the kitchen table: plain English, step by step, never condescending. If you have never touched a chatbot, you can start on page one of any of my books and keep up.

What I believe

  • Plain English first. If a sentence needs jargon to survive, the sentence failed.
  • Step by step. One thing at a time, with every click written out.
  • Never condescending. You are not behind; nobody told you yet. That is my job.
  • Local-first. Wherever possible, your files and your choices stay on your own computer.

Where the books come from

Asdale Dynamics, LLC is the small publishing house behind the books — an Indiana company focused on beginner-friendly AI education, open-source tools, and practical helpers for authors and small businesses. The website itself is one of those open-source templates, so what you are reading is also a starting point anyone can copy.

Education, never medical advice. Anything here about AI in health or caregiving is information only. I am a nurse, but a website does not know you like your care team does — medical decisions belong with you and your clinicians.
